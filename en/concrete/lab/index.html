Standards

How the tests on this page are carried out

  1. 01

    Slump test

    Measures the consistency of fresh concrete with a slump cone: fill the cone in layers, lift it straight up and measure how far the concrete settles. The target is set by the job’s designer or engineer.

    Awaiting confirmation
  2. 02

    Cube compressive strength

    Cubes cast from the same load are cured to a set age (for example 7 and 28 days) and crushed in the press. Results are reported in ksc with the age and specimen type. Cube and cylinder results are not directly comparable.

    Awaiting confirmation
  3. 03

    Cylinder compressive strength

    Used when the specification states cylinder strength. The procedure is similar to cubes, but the basis differs, so the specimen type is always shown.

    Awaiting confirmation
